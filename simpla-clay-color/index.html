From abstract to product

Every product starts as one grey lump. No colour yet, no edges, nothing to point at. We are comfortable there, and we do not rush out of it.

Research

The first colour goes in

Questions start coming out of it, more than you wanted. We talk to the people who will use it, watch what they actually do, and the loose ends stop scattering and fall into an order.

  • Brainstorm
  • Product research
  • UX research
  • Ideation

Wireframes

It takes a shape

Turning ideas into wireframes, mockups and prototypes. Structure before surface, so the argument is settled while it is still soft enough to change.

  • Prototyping
  • Product definitions
  • MVP
  • Consultation

Design

Where the colours meet

Crafting memorable, branded experiences. Your vision and flavour stay intact, usability comes first, and where the two press together a third tone appears that neither had alone.

  • Product design
  • UX and UI
  • Conceptual design
  • Style guide

Development

It starts to move

Leading and supporting development. We stay in it while it gets built, because the last ten percent is where products are won or lost.

  • Front end
  • UI development
  • Responsive components
  • HTML5

Live

And then it is a product

A value-centered product that leaves users feeling confident, engaged, and ready to take the next action. Same clay. Nothing was added but colour.

Work
