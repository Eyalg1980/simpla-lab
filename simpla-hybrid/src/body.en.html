Figma Weave

FIGMA WEAVE · ADOPTION IN ORGANIZATIONS

From a one-off prompt to a system your team runs on its own

Figma Weave, formerly Weavy, is a node-based platform for creating with AI, acquired by Figma. On one canvas you connect image, language and video models, editing tools and trained models. We bring it into design, marketing and video teams: we build your brand language with you, train a model on it, and leave you with apps that produce content in one click.

PROMPT TEMPLATE

Prompt template

A fixed structure with variables that keeps every run consistent. The visual language is written once, and written precisely.

LORA

Trained model

A LoRA that has learned your brand language and brings it back in every image, without explaining it again in every prompt.

WORKFLOW · APP

Workflow and app

Connects the two with more AI tools into one process, then wraps it in a simple interface anyone on the team can run.

Adoption in your organization

Six steps, side by side with your design and marketing teams. By the end, the team builds and maintains it on its own, with no outside dependency.
